## Versioning policy

The dataset follows semver, and its version is the `version` field in every API response.

- **Patch:** data changes (new, corrected or deactivated records).
- **Minor:** new optional fields or endpoints.
- **Major:** breaking changes, which also get a new `/vN` path.

## 1.0.0 — 21 November 2026

First release.

- Static `/v1` API: `all`, `banks`, `ewallets`, `bprs`, `by-id/{id}` and `meta`.
- {stats.total} records: {stats.banks} banks and {stats.ewallets} e-wallets, {stats.verified} of them `verified`. Plus {stats.bprs} BPR and BPRS in `/v1/bprs.json`.
- `bank_code` from OJK's "Sandi Bank"; names and BICs from Bank Indonesia's BI-FAST participant list.
- E-wallets have no bank code. An e-wallet becomes `verified` once its operator's licence is checked by hand on Bank Indonesia's register.
- Weekly sync with the BI-FAST, SKNBI and OJK lists and ASPI's QRIS directory, which also flags OJK renames and records ASPI no longer lists.
